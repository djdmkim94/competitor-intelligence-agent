Regulatory & policy signals

State and international AI regulation developments

Illinois — AI Safety Measures Act signed July 6; nation's most protective state AI law HIGH

Governor Pritzker signed the Illinois AI Safety Measures Act on July 6. Key provisions: model developers must publish AI risk frameworks, conduct annual independent third-party audits, and report incidents within 72 hours (24 hours for imminent harm threats). Effective January 1, 2028. Both OpenAI and Anthropic supported the bill; it passed with broad bipartisan support.

This is the first US state law to mandate AI audits. As a model for California and New York (cited in the legislation), it may become the de facto national standard if federal AI regulation remains stalled. Anthropic's safety-forward positioning gives it a compliance marketing first-mover advantage.
