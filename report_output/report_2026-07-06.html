Competitor feature launches

New capabilities from AI coding tools this week

Amazon (AWS) — Kiro: agentic IDE with spec-driven development New Competitor

Kiro is a full agentic IDE built on VS Code, powered by Amazon Bedrock, with "spec-driven development" as its core differentiator. Before writing any code, Kiro agents produce three structured files: requirements.md (user stories + acceptance criteria), design.md (architecture + data models + interfaces), and tasks.md (executable coding checklist). Agents then iterate against the spec. MCP support, steering rules for codebase-wide AI behavior, and 15+ language support are included. Currently free in preview.

Kiro positions itself explicitly as "the end of chaos-driven AI coding" — a direct response to criticism of prompt-driven coding tools. AWS enterprise relationships give it distribution into the same regulated enterprise accounts Codex targets, at zero acquisition cost. A seventh major player in the AI coding market forces Codex to more sharply articulate its distinct value.

Source: TechRadar · kiro.dev/blog · Constellation Research
