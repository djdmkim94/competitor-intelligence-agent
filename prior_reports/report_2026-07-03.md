## 2. ChatGPT Intelligence (PMM Lens)

### Competitor Messaging & Positioning

**Anthropic** — Claude Fable 5 returned July 1 to all global users on Pro, Max, Team, and select Enterprise plans. The redeployment follows a 19-day shutdown triggered by US export controls after Amazon researchers found a jailbreak allowing Fable 5 to surface software vulnerabilities. New safeguards include a cybersecurity classifier (99%+ blocking rate), a wider safety margin, accepted government conditions, and a HackerOne responsible disclosure channel. US Commerce Department's AI standards body (CAISI) called the safeguards "extraordinarily strong." Mythos 5 remains restricted to approved US organizations only.
- This is the strongest consumer-facing model in the market returning at scale — brand narrative shifts from "suspended" to "principled, audited, and now safer than anyone else."
- Through July 7, Fable 5 is included in 50% of weekly usage limits for Pro/Max/Team users, after which it requires usage credits — a scarcity mechanism that may drive Max upgrades.

**Anthropic** — Claude Science launched June 30, an AI workbench for scientists and pharmaceutical researchers. The product includes 60+ scientific databases and toolkits covering genomics, single-cell analysis, proteomics, structural biology, and cheminformatics. A parallel reviewer agent flags wrong citations and numbers in real-time. NVIDIA BioNeMo integration is bundled. Available in beta on macOS and Linux for Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans.
- This is Anthropic's first major vertical product beyond general assistant use — directly competing with specialized research tools rather than just ChatGPT. Pharma and biotech enterprise buyers are a new audience entirely.
- Anthropic is internally launching its own drug discovery program for neglected diseases, giving it first-party domain expertise to improve the product. Applications for $30K credits open through July 15.

**Google** — Gemini 3.5 Flash is now embedded in Google Search's "AI Mode" for all users globally, not just Gemini subscribers. The model is 4x faster than other frontier models and Google describes it as its strongest agentic/coding model to date. Gemini 3.5 Live Translate adds real-time speech-to-speech translation across 70+ languages.
- The integration into Google Search is the most significant consumer reach event this week — Gemini 3.5 is now the AI interface for billions of Google Search users without them needing to opt into a product, creating ambient AI exposure at a scale no competitor can match.

**xAI** — Grok 117M MAUs (as of March 2026 per SpaceX IPO filing), up from 35M in December. Grok Build 0.1 (coding agent) launched in API beta. grok-imagine-video-1.5-preview launched in API preview. Grok added to Databricks Agent Bricks. Grok 5 still unannounced as of July 3.
- Grok is growing its user base fast but the delayed Grok 5 creates an increasingly visible capability gap vs. Claude Fable 5, Gemini 3.5, and GPT-5.6. Every week without Grok 5 is a compounding disadvantage.
