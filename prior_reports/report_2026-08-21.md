The attack bypasses Grok's AI guardrails entirely because the guardrail scanner pattern-matches on plaintext and cannot detect encrypted payloads. The vulnerability was responsibly disclosed to xAI on June 3, 2026 — 79 days before public disclosure — and remains unpatched as of August 19. The attack was reproduced by Adversa AI on August 19 on `grok.com` running Grok 4.5 Fast. Media coverage is substantial: Hacker News, The Register, Dataconomy, SC Media, The Hacker News. Sub-signals: (a) The 79-day disclosure gap with no patch is damaging for xAI's enterprise credibility — enterprise security teams will flag this in vendor reviews; (b) The attack surface is `grok.com` web chat specifically (not the API); (c) The encrypted-payload approach may be replicable against other LLMs that perform decryption on user-provided content — watch for copycat disclosures. Context: a separate Grok Lite service glitch on August 20 (nonsensical text responses) is a distinct, lower-severity incident.
   - Confidence: HIGH on vulnerability existence and mechanics (Adversa AI researcher report, Hacker News, The Register, Dataconomy, SC Media, The Hacker News — Aug 20–21, multiple consistent sources). HIGH on 79-day disclosure gap (reported June 3, disclosed Aug 20). HIGH on ongoing unpatched status (Adversa AI confirmed reproduction Aug 19). MEDIUM on enterprise impact (web chat only; API not confirmed affected).
